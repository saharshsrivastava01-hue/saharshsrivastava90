FAQ

Before we begin

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on scope, features and timeline. Share a brief and budget range; a written estimate can be prepared before work begins.

How long does delivery take?

A small landing page may take days, while a custom application takes longer. A realistic timeline is agreed after requirements are reviewed.

Is the AI demo production-ready?

No. The current website contains an illustrative frontend demo only. A real AI service requires backend development, security controls, testing and appropriate model integration.

How is client information handled?

Do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive records through this enquiry form. Data-handling requirements should be agreed before any project files are shared.
