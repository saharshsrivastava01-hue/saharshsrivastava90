# NEXUS AI Studio — Client Acquisition & Delivery Playbook

**Goal:** Win and deliver the first paid small-business website project, then repeat with a documented process.
**Operating rule:** Never claim a client, portfolio result, certification, or technical capability that has not been verified.

## Offer to sell first

**Mobile-friendly business website**
- One-page or up to five-page website, depending on agreed scope.
- Responsive layout for mobile and desktop.
- Click-to-call, WhatsApp enquiry link, map/social links where supplied.
- Contact form only when a working form backend is configured and tested; otherwise use a clear email/WhatsApp link.
- Basic page titles, descriptions, accessibility checks, and launch checklist.
- Client supplies logo, copy, images, business details, and legal/privacy wording unless separately agreed.

**Do not promise:** guaranteed sales, SEO rankings, custom AI agents, payment processing, complex dashboards, unlimited revisions, or a delivery date before requirements are reviewed.

## Commercial starter scope

Use a written quote after discovery. Suggested test pricing for a beginner portfolio-building offer (adjust to scope and local market):
- Single landing page: ₹2,500–₹5,000.
- Small 3–5 page brochure site: ₹5,000–₹12,000.
- Maintenance: separately quoted monthly.

These are suggested starting ranges, not market guarantees. Include hosting/domain charges separately and obtain client approval before any paid purchase. Suggested payment milestones: 40% advance, 40% on preview approval, 20% before handover. For small projects, agree a simpler milestone in writing.

## 20-stage operating workflow

1. Choose one niche at a time: local clinics, tutors, restaurants, salons, repair services, consultants, or small B2B firms.
2. Define one clear outcome: make it easier for customers to understand the service and enquire.
3. Review the NEXUS website and demo; label demo work accurately.
4. Prepare one niche-specific sample landing page with fictional sample data clearly marked as a demo.
5. Create a concise service one-pager and transparent pricing ranges.
6. Build a prospect list from public business websites/directories; record source and date.
7. Prioritize businesses with an outdated site, broken mobile layout, unclear contact path, or no site.
8. Verify each prospect's business name and public business contact channel.
9. Do not scrape private data, buy dubious lists, or bulk-spam.
10. Write one personalized observation per prospect; do not fabricate an audit.
11. Send a short, relevant email or use an appropriate public business enquiry channel.
12. For WhatsApp, contact only where the business has published that channel for enquiries or consented; keep it relevant and low-volume.
13. Follow up once after 4–5 business days if no response; stop if asked.
14. Ask discovery questions: goal, audience, pages, features, content readiness, examples, budget, deadline, decision-maker.
15. Send a written scope, exclusions, timeline, price, payment milestones, and revision limit.
16. Start only after written acceptance and agreed advance payment.
17. Build a staging preview; do not use client passwords in chat or commit secrets to Git.
18. Test mobile/desktop, links, forms, accessibility basics, spelling, and performance.
19. Obtain client approval, collect balance, transfer ownership/access securely, and provide handover notes.
20. Request honest feedback and permission before showing the work publicly; ask for a referral only after delivery.

## Prospect qualification score (0–10)

- Real business and verifiable public presence: 0–2
- Clear website problem we can solve: 0–2
- Public business contact route: 0–2
- Service fit with current skills: 0–2
- Plausible budget/timing signal: 0–2

Prioritize 7–10. Do not infer budget from appearance or personal characteristics.

## Daily execution target

- Research 10 relevant businesses.
- Select 5 well-matched prospects.
- Send 3–5 individually personalized messages (not bulk blasts).
- Record all responses and next actions.
- Spend a separate block improving one demo or delivery skill.
Track: researched, qualified, contacted, replies, discovery calls, quotes, wins, revenue, delivery hours.

## Discovery call script

1. “What do you want the website to help customers do?”
2. “Which services and pages should it include?”
3. “Do you already have a domain, hosting, logo and content?”
4. “Are there examples you like?”
5. “What is your preferred launch date and budget range?”
6. Repeat the scope back and confirm the next step. Do not promise work outside the agreed scope.

## Project acceptance checklist

- Client identity and decision-maker confirmed.
- Written requirements and deliverables.
- Price, taxes/fees if applicable, and payment milestones.
- Timeline starts after required content and advance.
- Number of revisions and change-request pricing.
- Domain/hosting ownership and renewal responsibility.
- Privacy, cookie, contact-form and data-handling responsibilities agreed.
- Written acceptance received before starting.

## AI-assisted delivery process

AI can draft copy, create page structure, generate code, explain errors, write test cases, and prepare handover documents. A human must verify facts, test code, protect client data, and approve the final deliverable. Do not let an agent send client messages, commit code, spend money, or publish changes without appropriate review.
